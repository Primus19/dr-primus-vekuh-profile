Dr. Primus Vekuh

Adjunct Professor of Computer Science, University of the Cumberlands | PhD, Information Technology | Founder, Primus Learning | Researcher in cloud computing, AI and SME technology adoption

Conroe, Texas, United States

Dr. Primus Vekuh is an Adjunct Professor of Computer Science at the University of the Cumberlands, where he teaches Advanced Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Operating Systems at graduate level. He earned his PhD in Information Technology from the University of the Cumberlands (2024); his dissertation studied cloud computing adoption, user experience and performance in small and medium-sized enterprises in Cameroon. He is the founder of Primus Learning and leads Primus Cloud Solutions, and he serves as a dissertation chair and committee member at Capitol Technology University.
