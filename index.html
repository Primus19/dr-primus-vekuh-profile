About

Building the Future,
One Innovation at a Time

Dr. Primus Vekuh is a transformative technology leader with a Ph.D. in Information Technology, over 12 years of enterprise experience across multiple Fortune 500 companies, and an unwavering passion for pushing the boundaries of what technology can achieve. He has held senior leadership roles including Senior Cloud Manager, Senior DevOps Manager, Senior Staff Infrastructure Engineer, and Senior Solutions Architect at some of the world's largest consulting firms and enterprises — As an Adjunct Professor at the University of the Cumberlands, he shapes the next generation of AI and computing professionals. As a serial entrepreneur, he builds products that solve real-world problems across AI, prediction markets, fintech, cloud computing, and education technology.

Originally from Cameroon, Primus has built a global footprint spanning the United States, Africa, and Europe, mentoring and training over 1,000 students and professionals across 10+ countries into successful tech careers. He has personally guided hundreds of aspiring engineers into cloud, DevOps, AI, and cybersecurity roles. His mission is clear: democratize technology and empower the next generation of innovators.
